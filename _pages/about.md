<br /><br />
 I am a third year PhD student in statistics at the [University of Michigan](https://lsa.umich.edu/stats). I am co-advised by [Tailen Hsing](https://dept.stat.lsa.umich.edu/~thsing/) and [Kean Ming Tan](http://www.keanmingtan.com/). 
 Before this, I completed my M. Math and B. Math (Hons.) degree at [Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore](https://www.isibang.ac.in/).
<br /><br />

I work in functional data analysis, a branch of statistics that deals with continuous functions or curves.
I am also interested in operator learning, a branch of machine learning where the goal is to learn maps between function spaces.
<br /><br />

 Outside of research, I spend my time cooking, baking and painting.
